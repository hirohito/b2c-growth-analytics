## Referral & Virality Benchmarks

### K-Factor

- Viral product: K > 1.0 (rare, world-class)
- Strong supplementary virality: K = 0.3–0.7
- Weak virality: K = 0.1–0.3
- Effectively no virality: K < 0.1

### NPS (Net Promoter Score) — Consumer Apps

| Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| > 70 | World-class (Apple, Netflix territory) |
| 50–70 | Strong |
| 30–50 | Good |
| 10–30 | Average — meaningful work needed |
| 0–10 | Weak |
| < 0 | Problem — more detractors than promoters |

### Referral Rate (% of MAU who refer ≥1 person/month)

- Strong: > 8%
- Average: 3–7%
- Weak: < 2%

## Quick Diagnostic Rules of Thumb

When reviewing a B2C product's metrics, flag these red lines:

🔴 **CRITICAL — Stop & Fix Before Scaling**
- D30 retention < 5% (any vertical)
- Quick Ratio < 1.0 (shrinking)
- LTV/CAC < 1.0 (burning money)
- Activation rate < 15% (broken onboarding)
- Monthly churn > 15% (subscription)

🟡 **CAUTION — Needs Optimization**
- D30 retention in bottom quartile of vertical
- Quick Ratio between 1.0–1.5
- LTV/CAC between 1.0–2.5
- Payback period > 18 months
- DAU/MAU well below vertical median

🟢 **HEALTHY — Scale With Confidence**
- D30 retention in top quartile of vertical
- Quick Ratio > 3
- LTV/CAC > 3
- Payback period < 6 months
- Power user tier (L21+) > 15% of MAU
